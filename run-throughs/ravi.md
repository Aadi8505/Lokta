## 2. Four Outputs Produced

### O1: Verdict
- **Decision**: **`Borrow` (with recommendation to compare LAP vs. Unsecured)**
- **Reason**: *"An unsecured loan of ₹15,00,000 would stretch your monthly obligations too high (over 90% FOIR). However, using your shop premises collateral for a secured loan / LAP brings the EMI into a comfortable range (25%–35% FOIR)."*
- **Supporting Reasons**:
  - The business expansion is productive, generating expected incremental income.
  - ₹45L unencumbered property provides prime security, enabling long tenure and low interest.

### O2: Maximum Amount
- **Product A (Unsecured Business Loan)**:
  - Max Lender Sanction: ₹6,50,000 – ₹8,00,000 (constrained by ₹35k ITR income)
  - Requested ₹15L is **NOT viable** under unsecured credit alone.
- **Product B (Loan Against Property - LAP)**:
  - Max Lender Sanction: ₹22,00,000 – ₹27,00,000 (backed by 60% LTV of ₹45L property)
  - Borrower Safe Carry: **₹18,00,000 – ₹24,00,000**
- **Recommendation**: Use **LAP / Secured Business Financing** for the ₹15L loan.

### O3: Fair Interest Rate & True APR
- **Unsecured Business Loan**: 16.0% – 28.0% (Wide unknown score spread, high cost)
- **LAP / Secured Business Loan**: **9.50% – 12.00%** (Nominal rate)
- **True APR (IRR)**: **10.25% – 13.10%** (incorporating 1.0% processing fee amortized over 7–10 years)
- **Insight**: Pledging shop collateral saves Ravi over ₹4,50,000 in interest over the loan life.

### O4: Safe EMI Ceiling & Stress Test
- **Safe EMI Ceiling**: **₹41,500 / month** (incorporates living costs + savings buffer + productive income credit)
- **Recommended Tenure**: **120 months (10 years) LAP** (Expected EMI: ₹19,800 – ₹21,500/mo)
- **Stress Test**:
  - **Income Drop (-20% shock)**: Stressed safe EMI remains ₹30,700/mo, easily servicing the ₹19.8k EMI.
  - **Rate Rise (+200 bps floating reset)**: EMI rises to ~₹21,600/mo, remaining well under the ₹41,500 safe ceiling.
